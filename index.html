        What to do

        About Ready Map

        Ready Map is part of Ready, an emergency-prep helper. It shows where you are, lets you save places, finds help nearby, and shows weather warnings.

        Your privacy

        • No tracking. No ads. No analytics. No cookies.
        • Your GPS location and your saved places are stored only on this device. There is no Ready Map server, so nothing is sent to us.
        • Two features send a spot to an outside service, and only when you use them: "Show on this map" (nearby places) sends that spot's coordinates to the Overpass API, and "Weather warnings" sends your location (or the map center) to the National Weather Service.
        • The app only talks to these services:
          • OpenStreetMap, for map pictures (tiles).
          • Overpass API, only when you tap "Show on this map" for nearby places.
          • NOAA / National Weather Service, only when you turn on a weather layer.
          • RainViewer, only if NOAA radar does not work.
          • Google Maps or Apple Maps, only when you tap one of those links.
        • When you use those services, they can see a map area or spot, like any website.

        GPS needs a secure page

        Phones only share GPS with pages that start with https:// (or localhost for testing).

        For bots: link to a spot

        Add these to the link: lat, lon, and if you want, label and zoom (1 to 19).

        ./?lat=40.7608&lon=-111.891&label=Meeting%20spot&zoom=15
